support.witloom@gmail.com

Having trouble?

Before contacting support, you can try:

  • Closing and reopening Witloom.
  • Checking that your device is running a supported version of iOS or iPadOS.
  • Checking your internet connection for features that retrieve online content.
  • Checking microphone and speech-recognition permissions if Drive Mode cannot hear your answers.
  • Making sure your device volume is turned up for audio-based games.

If the issue continues, email us and include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and a short description of what happened.
