Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

Overview

Witloom is designed to provide trivia, knowledge, word, geography, music, and estimation games without requiring users to create an account. This Privacy Policy explains how Witloom handles information and device permissions.

Information Stored on Your Device

Witloom may store app preferences, settings, gameplay progress, statistics, streaks, and similar game information locally on your device so that the app can remember your preferences and progress.

This locally stored information is used to provide app functionality and is not used by Witloom for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Drive Mode can use your device's microphone and speech-recognition capabilities so you can answer trivia questions using your voice. Witloom requests these permissions only when they are needed for features that use them.

Speech-recognition functionality may rely on services provided by the operating system. The handling of information by those services is subject to the applicable platform provider's terms and privacy practices.

Photo Library

Witloom may request access to your photo library only if you choose to use a feature that requires interaction with photos. Witloom does not use photo-library access for advertising or tracking.

Online Content and Third-Party Services

Some Witloom features may communicate with third-party or platform services to provide online content or functionality. For example, music-related gameplay may retrieve song preview content, and online trivia content may be retrieved from an external content service.

Those services may receive technical information ordinarily transmitted when a device makes an internet request, such as an IP address or basic request information. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy practices.

Advertising and Tracking

Witloom does not contain third-party advertising and does not use your activity for cross-app or cross-website advertising tracking.

Accounts

Witloom does not require a Witloom account or user login to play.

Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information and preferences stored locally by Witloom remain on your device until they are changed, cleared through available app or device controls, or the app and its associated local data are removed.

Because Witloom does not require a user account, there is no Witloom account profile to delete. If you have a privacy question or believe you have provided information directly to Witloom Support that you would like deleted, contact us using the email address below.

Your Choices

You can manage permissions such as microphone, speech recognition, and photo access through your device's privacy settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related optional feature from working.

Children's Privacy

Witloom does not knowingly use the app to collect personal information from children for advertising or tracking purposes.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Witloom changes. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
